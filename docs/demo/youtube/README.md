The YouTube kit of the contest videos (30 Oct 2026), copied as it was from the author's walkthrough folder: the text that was published, and the scripts that made the thumbnails. The videos and the thumbnail pictures are not in the repo.

- `metadata.md`: titles, tags, category, thumbnails and the publishing notes of the demo (youtu.be/x_zMu29HfDA), the full walkthrough (youtu.be/xpUb5XcHtlQ) and the "Glitch Out" music video (youtu.be/w6CTnHRR8U0).
- `demo_description.txt`, `long_description.txt`, `song_description.txt`: the descriptions. `FULL_WALKTHROUGH_URL` and `DEMO_VIDEO_URL` in the first two were filled with the links above when the videos were up.
- `make_thumbs_xf.py` (the demo's and the walkthrough's), `make_thumb_song.py` (the song's two), `make_cover_916.py` (the 9:16 cover X shows as the first frame): Pillow, 1280x720 or 1080x1920, the product's own type (Space Grotesk, from `src/xenoform/ui_static/fonts`, woff2 converted to ttf in a `fonts/` folder beside them) over a frame of the montage moved aside on its own mirrored ground. They read frames from a `frames/` folder (`scarab_11.0.png`, `dragon_0.4.png`, `song_jelly_2.5.png`, `song_gold_229.6.png`) taken from the cuts named in their docstrings; those frames are not kept.
