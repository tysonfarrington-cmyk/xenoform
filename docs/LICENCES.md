## The free film pack's sources

Version 2 of `configs/film_pack.json` (1 November 2026) cuts 40 clips from 29 sources. Nothing of them is in the repo: Setup & models reads each clip's window from the file named here (a byte-range read, a few MB a clip; the file's size is what a whole download would cost) into `work/ui/film_pack/`, and writes the same lines to `work/ui/film_pack/CREDITS.md`. Every source is one of three kinds:

- **Prelinger Archives items that carry the Creative Commons Public Domain Dedication** (14). The archive's own rights page says that not every Prelinger film is in the public domain and that the dedication governs the films it is affixed to, so only items whose archive.org licence is Public Domain were taken; items of the collection without it were left out, however good their footage.
- **Works of the United States government** (12): NASA's videos, from NASA's own Image and Video Library or NASA's collections on archive.org (NASA material is not protected by copyright in the United States; no NASA endorsement is implied and no NASA insignia is used), and Air Force and other government films from the National Archives (FedFlix) and the Air Force Now series.
- **Films published before 1929** (3): Metropolis (1927), A Trip to the Moon (1902) and Steamboat Willie (1928), each from an archive.org item that is marked public domain. The first pack's copies of these, whose pages state no licence, were replaced by these.

Nothing is taken from YouTube, Pexels, Pixabay or any source under another licence. The General and Battleship Potemkin, in the first pack, are out (1 November 2026), and the taste file's `banned_clips` keeps their clips out of any cut.
