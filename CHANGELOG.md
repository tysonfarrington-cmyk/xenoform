## 0.3.3 (unreleased, the week to the contest)

- **The example theme is underwater, not racing** (Tys, 29 Oct: "you want to see cars driving not morphing"). The underwater theme is sea life only now (a jellyfish, an octopus, a seahorse, a nautilus, a manta ray and ten more: soft, flowing forms are what a morph does best; the diving helmet, anchor, ship's wheel, submarine and porthole went), and twelve of its fifteen are already in the library. A theme's own name typed alone is that theme even where it is also a place word: "underwater", "space" and "the wild west" read as scenes before. The desk's placeholder, examples and the README lead with it; racing stays in the list.
- **A click no longer blinks the desk** (found recording the walkthrough, 29 Oct; Tys, 28 Oct: "make sure everything is static"). Every click redraws the desk, and the fade-in on the page faded the whole desk out and back with a 4 px slide each time. It plays now only when the screen changes (the desk, the making screen, the cut, a tool page).
- **None, a vibe of no vibe** (Tys, 29 Oct: "lets make sure in vibes we have an option for none as well"). The last featured card: each object in its own real materials and colours, plain dark grey backdrop, soft studio light, photoreal, a light glow and no dance look. It ships twelve stills and its own library strip like the other featured vibes, and its library on this machine holds 78 checked stills from every subject family (eight culled by eye: plain shapes, a card house, a wisp).
- **Clips or objects, never both** (Tys, 28 Oct: "if we are going to morph movie clips, make it so its just movie clips, and if we are doing objects just objects"). A **Morph** row at the top of the Vibe step: **Objects** or **Your clips**. A clips cut (`xenoform.clip_cut`) alternates a clip and a morph on the song's shots: each clip plays its busiest stretch at 1:1, each morph is generated from the last frame the clip before shows into the first frame of the next, on the cut's tier (Draft, Fast Final or Final, which now takes the frames as they are). Its draft before the render is the clips cut to the beat, never the vibe's stills. An object cut never takes a clip (`ui._apply_own`: the clips stay out whatever `cut_in_clips` says), so the Work in progress switch that cut clips into an object chain is gone; the idea line, the library, the Chain row, Scenes and Foreshadow step aside in clips mode.
- **The timeline, simpler** (Tys: "the time line is a bit confusing to use"). Tiles are the cut's own frames, numbered; the seams are handles on the tiles; one drawer for a shot in four tabs (Shot, Look, Join, Regenerate) with ◀ ▶ to step through the shots; one line of help a tab instead of a paragraph a row. The duplicate `/api/videos/{id}/shots` route is gone.

- **A chain keeps to its theme** (Tys, 28 Oct, on "trophy → pearl → icicle → rally car → ... → wishing well → peony → snare drum → sundial ...": "this sequence doesnt make sense.. your generations should have a proper theme"). Three causes, three fixes. Your own shapes led every cut of their vibe for good, so three stills regrown for one line opened every neon-noir cut after it: they lead now only when the line, its theme or Your own shapes names them (the Make body carries `own_shapes`), and are ordinary stills of the pool otherwise. A theme brought five things, so ten of fifteen morphs fell back to the vibe's whole pool: every one of the 33 themes has fifteen things now (no brand, no text, no place word), grown on first use (about 80 s), and a theme or a family the line names carries the chain alone, walked again rather than topped up with unrelated objects (`THEME_CHAIN_MIN`). And a cut with no line wandered the library: a vibe with forty or more stills now cuts within one realm of related families (creatures, garden, cosmos, machines, dark fantasy, home and craft; `stills.REALMS`), the one it has used least, family by family.

The timeline's shot drawer has Hue, Saturation, Brightness, Contrast and Warmth (`effects.grade`, one 3x3 matrix a frame) and its own Effects (As the cut, or its own set of Dither hits, Feedback, Kaleido, Slit-scan), for Apply with the rest.
- **Move cuts, reorder, duplicate, delete** (Tys: "trim and move cuts", "reorder, delete, duplicate"). A boundary's **Move the cut** steps it a beat or half a beat either way (`shift`, at most 4 s, never leaving a shot under six frames); a shot's **Order** row moves its morph left or right (the slots stay on the beat, the morphs swap), duplicates it into the next slot, or deletes it (the shot before plays on through its time; a striped sliver marks the place and restores it). All on the cached morphs, no GPU (`dense.arrange_shots`); the timeline keeps one row a plan shot, so its indices hold through a delete.

- **Dream this span** (Tys, 28 Oct: "Build Dream this span on the timeline: Z-Image Turbo img2img over a chosen 1 to 3 s, at 12 fps, cut in like a generated run"). `xenoform.dream` with `workflows/zimage_i2i_api.json`: the timeline's shot drawer has **Dream here** (1, 2 or 3 s, light or deep: denoise 0.35 or 0.55); the span is whole shots from the one clicked (Polly and your clips stop it; a generated run or an earlier dream can be dreamed over); every other output frame of each shot, as the shot plays its clip, goes through Z-Image Turbo img2img at the clip's aspect (432 short side on Draft, 576 on Final), one seed for the run and the vibe's look in the prompt; each dream is held for two frames, the second a half blend into the next, and the shot's clip is replaced by its dream played 1:1 (`dream` on the shots, `dreams` in the record); the cut re-composes with the knobs, Look and effects on top; the joins into and out of the span are cuts, the lane draws a Dream bar, and a dreamed shot is left alone by swaps, re-renders and the morph timing. On the proof cut (9207df6b) 2 s from 7 s (24 dreamed frames) took 72 s of GPU and 110 s in all with the re-cut. `POST /api/jobs/{id}/dream`; `tests/test_dream.py` pins it.
- **Dither and TouchDesigner-style effects; the blocky smear made opt-in** (Tys, 28 Oct: "a proper dither effect ... the pixelated effect we have should be an option but something optional or used sparingly ... more touch designer type effects ... stream diffusion, if it's possible and not too much work"). `xenoform.effects`: a **Dither** Look (the whole cut mapped onto four tones measured from the cut's own clips, `palette_from_clips`, each band's most saturated colour; the steps broken up by a 64x64 blue-noise threshold map made by void-and-cluster, so the dots never clump or grid the way Bayer or error diffusion do; the light range eased within a shot; a new grain on each beat; a dot of 2 px at 540x960 and 4 at 1080x1920) and an **Effects** row of four effects that run after the Look in the compose, any together, riding the kick and the loudness under the Intensity knob: **Dither hits**, **Feedback** (lighten-blended zooming tunnel), **Kaleido** (mirrored wedges, 6, or 8 past Chaos 0.6, a step on each beat) and **Slit-scan** (each row from a moment further back, blended between frames). All CPU and all re-cuttable (the re-cut sends `effects`; about 30 to 65 s on the 12 s demo). The blocky **smear** handoff is off by default now (`boundary_fx.pick(smear=)`: "off", "sparing" at one handoff in six and never the drop, "on"); the Effects row's **Blocky smear** chip brings it back sparingly, and while it is off a vibe's datamosh transition is a chromatic split. Recipe codes carry the effects (`ef`). StreamDiffusion: not on this machine (no SD-Turbo-class checkpoint, no StreamDiffusion nodes; installing them needs downloads and a ComfyUI restart). The nearest thing with what is installed, Z-Image Turbo img2img over a cut's frames, measured 2.55 s a frame warm at 432x768 (46 s for the first) and re-dreams the object on every frame: an offline "dream" span is possible (about two minutes of GPU for two seconds), live is not. `tests/test_effects.py` pins it.
- **Morph timing: the change lands on the beat** (docs/MORPH_ENGINE_PLAN.md E2; Tys, 28 Oct: "time each morph so the change lands on the beat (Smooth, On the beat, Snap), on the cached morphs"). The cut put every handoff on the beat, but inside a shot the object changed whenever the model started changing it. `xenoform.morph_timing` measures each cached clip's change frame by frame (`progress`: 0 at still A, 1 at still B), says how far along the change each output frame should be (`target`) and plays the source frame that far along (`cloud_morph.shot_indices(progress=)`); a slow stretch blends the two frames either side instead of repeating one and jumping (`beatfx._blend_at`). **Smooth** is the even play as before; **On the beat** moves the change in steps, fastest on each beat inside the shot; **Snap** holds the object and changes it in about a beat on the shot's downbeat, a quarter beat into the drop and sharper there. Every shot still starts on still A and ends on still B, the stutters, holds, skips and reverses act on the re-timed frames, and a held shot, Polly and your clips are left alone. A **Timing** row sits under Morph on the desk and beside the knobs on the cut's screen (**Re-cut with these knobs** sends `morph_timing`, no GPU); the timeline's shot drawer gives a shot its own (`shot_overrides[i].timing`, applied with Apply); the record keeps `morph_timing` and `timed`; recipe codes carry it (`mt`). On the proof cut (9207df6b, the demo in neon-noir) each re-cut took about 40 s, and the median share of a morph's change within an eighth of a second of its downbeat went from 31 % (Smooth) to 43 % (On the beat) and 47 % (Snap). Fixed on the way: **Re-cut with these knobs** answered "No such video." on a cut reopened after a desk restart (the route now registers it from its folder, as the timeline's edits do). `tests/test_morph_timing.py` pins it.
- **Morph styles: how A becomes B** (docs/MORPH_ENGINE_PLAN.md E1; Tys, 28 Oct: "Ship Shatter, Bloom, Spin and Fold beside Melt"). Every morph ran on one prompt, so every transformation was a melt. `xenoform.morph_style` gives the prompt's opening five ways: **Melt** (the old prompt, word for word), **Shatter** (shards that swirl and snap together), **Bloom** (opens like a flower, the next growing out of its centre), **Spin** (turns as it changes, the most motion) and **Fold** (creases like paper); a pair that touches a scene keeps the scene prompt. The desk has a **Morph** row under the knobs: **Auto** melts through the verses and shatters on the drop, blooms the opening under Chaos 0.6 and from there folds the builds and spins every other loud shot after the drop (all Melt at Chaos 0.15 or less), or one style for every morph. Draft (`cloud_morph._local_clips(styles=)`), Final (`final_tier.final_clips(styles=)`) and the Final's Draft fallback take a style a shot; the record keeps `morph_style` and `morph_styles` (one a shot), the lane gives each shot its `style` and the choices, and the timeline's shot drawer has the same chips: another style re-renders that one morph (`dense.rerender_morph(style=)`, `POST /api/jobs/{id}/rerender` with `style`; with none given a re-render keeps the shot's own). Swaps keep the shot's style, a generated run takes the cut's setting, **Render at Final** carries the prototype's style a shot (when the Final plans the same shots), and recipe codes carry the setting (`ms`). The tile of a shot that is not a Melt names its style. Fixed on the way: a Final's fallback notes replaced the cut's notes instead of joining them (the grown-for-the-line note was lost). `tests/test_morph_style.py` pins it.
