Xenoform

Every drop is a transformation.

A music video director, not a visualiser.

Xenoform turns a song into a music video where one object keeps transforming into the next, every change landing on the beat. Free, local, open weights.

One desk: a song and a vibe make a quick prototype (a Draft of 30 s around the drop for a long song: a stills draft in seconds, LTX morphs on your own GPU in minutes); refine it on its timeline (each shot's object, look and FX, each handoff, a morph re-rendered, a run generated), re-cut it with the knobs without the GPU, then render the whole song or Final and share it. 9:16, Dense, Draft and the house look by default, with Flow, 16:9 and 1:1, the Look presets, stem reactions, Polly, your own footage and recipe codes under Fine-tune. Any AI can drive the same desk over MCP (… → Connect an AI). Live visuals run at /vj (a MIDI controller drives the macros on both pages); TouchDesigner gets OSC from the xenoform osc command.

How the cut is made

The desk pings /system_stats on this address. ComfyUI on this machine is http://127.0.0.1:8188.

LTX-2.3 is the default core. LTX-2.5 is local open weights under the LTX-2.x Community License, free under $10M annual revenue. If that distilled file is missing, or the open-weights check fails, the cut stays on LTX-2.3. Turbo stays on LTX 0.9.5, and an 8 GB card stays on 0.9.5 unless you pick 2.5. Partner nodes stay out.

Make renders through ComfyUI at this address (the Comfy SDK, through the local proxy on port 8189) whenever it answers; when it does not, Make plays the vibe's pictures in the browser and says so.

Off by default. This covers FLUX 3 API, H3 API, partner nodes, and cloud LLMs. The demo, the judges page, and setup never need it.

Off by default. On, Fine-tune shows Cloud quality: LTX-2.5 morphs rendered by a paid Comfy partner node, billed to your Comfy account after a price card and a confirm click. The default path is 100% open weights and never needs this.
